How it works

  1. 1
    We bring you ideas that already worked

    Not brainstormed. Each one is read off a video that performed in a market like yours, then ranked against what has actually won views and enquiries for the businesses on our roster.

  2. 2
    You pick the ones you want

    Three or four a month, with the title and thumbnail a viewer would see. Anything that is not for you, you drop, and it never comes back.

  3. 3
    We interview you, then write it

    A few questions only you can answer — your market, your clients, what you have seen. Nothing factual in the script comes from anywhere else, so it never invents a figure about the place you work.

  4. 4
    You film. We do the rest

    Editing, thumbnail, description, posting, and a tracking link on every video so an enquiry can be traced to the one that caused it.

What we ask for on your channel

Connecting YouTube is optional and you can disconnect at any time. When you do connect it, we ask for exactly three things:

  • See your videos and how many views and comments they get
  • See who watched — roughly where they are, and how long they stayed
  • Upload a video when you ask us to post one

We cannot read your email, your files, or anything else on your Google account. Nothing is ever posted without you asking for it. The full detail is in our privacy policy.
