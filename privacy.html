Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

First Agent Studio is a service for producing YouTube videos and handling enquiries on behalf of real estate agents and other local businesses. It is operated by Leadific LLC, trading as First Agent Studio, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, which is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy covers the personal data we hold, why we hold it, who processes it for us, and what we do with the Google account data our clients choose to connect.

We are contactable at hello@firstagentstudio.com, which is also the address to use for any request below.

Who this covers

Our clients are businesses that engage us to produce videos, and the people on their team who use our app. We also hold details for people who make an enquiry through a page we run for a client, such as a neighbourhood quiz (we call them “enquirers” below). For enquirers, we act on behalf of the client whose page they used. We do not sell anything to consumers, we do not sell personal data, and we do not operate an advertising network.

What we collect

  • Account details — name, email address and business details of the people who use the service.
  • Material you give us — transcripts of videos you have made, notes you write about your market, photographs for thumbnails, and video footage.
  • Enquiry details — when somebody contacts one of our clients through a page we run: their name, email, phone number, their answers (for example the kind of home and area they are interested in), the video or link that brought them there, and the messages and calls exchanged with the client.
  • Consent records — when an enquirer agrees to be contacted by text or phone, we store exactly what they agreed to, the wording shown, the time, and their IP address. We only text or call people who have given that consent, and anyone can reply STOP to end texts at any time.
  • Usage and technical data — basic logs, error reports and anonymous usage statistics so we can keep the service working.
  • Google account data — only what is described in the next section, and only if you connect an account.

Text messages (SMS)

When you apply to work with us, our application form asks for your mobile number and shows a checkbox, unticked until you tick it, that reads: “I agree to receive texts and calls from First Agent Studio about my application and my account. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.” Ticking it is optional and is not a condition of applying; without it we only email you about your application. When you tick it we store the exact wording you agreed to, its version, the time and your IP address. We use your number only to send the messages that wording describes, and you can reply STOP to any text to stop them.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. A client may connect their own YouTube channel. We ask for two read-only permissions and use each of them for one thing:

  • youtube.readonly — to list the videos on your channel and read their public view, like and comment counts, so we can show you how each video we made is performing.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read audience statistics for your channel, such as approximate location and how long people watched, so we can describe who is actually watching and choose better video ideas for you.

Neither permission lets us upload, edit or delete anything on your channel. If we add a connection in future (for example, copying finished videos to your own Google Drive), we will update this policy and ask for that permission separately.

Limited Use

First Agent Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we do not use Google user data to serve advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the service you asked for, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit permission, where it is necessary for security purposes or to comply with the law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymised.

Artificial intelligence

We use Anthropic’s Claude models to draft scripts and video plans. Material you give us — your transcripts, your notes, your answers to our questions — is sent to that service to produce them. It is not used to train any model. Google user data obtained through the permissions above is not sent to any AI model.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the job named here: Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (file storage and site security checks), Vercel (hosting), Twilio (texts, calls and phone verification), Postmark (email), Inngest (background jobs), Anthropic (AI drafting, as described above), Sentry (error reports), PostHog (anonymous usage statistics) and Stripe (payments from our clients). They process data on our instructions and not for their own purposes.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Access tokens for Google accounts are encrypted before they are stored.

  • Video files — raw footage, finished videos and short clips are kept for 90 days after the video is published (or 90 days after upload if never used), then deleted. Clients are reminded before this happens and can download everything at any time.
  • Account and brand material — headshots, logos, thumbnail templates, scripts and plans are kept for as long as we work together.
  • Enquiry details — kept for as long as the client uses our service, and exported to the client if they leave. Opt-out (STOP) records are kept so we never contact that number again.
  • When a client leaves, they have 30 days to download anything still stored, after which it is removed. Ask us and we will delete data sooner, unless we must keep it by law.

Cookies

Our pages use only the cookies needed to run them, plus one optional cookie that remembers which link brought you to us for 30 days. We ask before setting the optional one. We do not use advertising cookies.

Disconnecting and deleting

You can disconnect your YouTube channel at any time from your settings page, which removes our stored access immediately. You can also revoke it directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your account and everything in it deleted, email us and we will do it and confirm when it is done.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address at the top and we will respond within thirty days. If you made an enquiry through one of our clients’ pages, you can also contact that client directly, and reply STOP to any text to stop texts immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we do with your data, we will tell clients directly rather than only updating this page.
